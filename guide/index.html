How to use Pordl Pitching

A quick guide for parents and coaches. The app answers one question every day: can he pitch today, and how much?

Get started

  1. Create an account. Tap Create an account, enter your email and a password, confirm from the email we send, then sign in. Want to look first? Tap See a demo. Nothing in the demo is saved.
  2. Add it to your home screen. iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.
  3. Set up your pitcher. On the Pitchers tab, enter his name, age group, starter or reliever, season and teams. Check He also plays catcher if he does. Tap Save profile.
  4. Add his last break from throwing under Time off from throwing on the same tab.
  5. More than one pitcher? Use Add another pitcher. Switch between them with the name buttons at the top.

Your data syncs to every phone, tablet or computer you sign in on.

The status card

The big colored card at the top tells you if he can pitch today. Check it before every game.

Green: Ready to pitch. Fully rested. Aim for the Pordl target.
Yellow: Ready, with care. He can pitch, but read the warning (pitched yesterday, recent soreness, high innings, several teams, or still ramping up).
Red: Rest. He shouldn't pitch today. The card says why and when he can pitch again.

Pordl target vs. Pitch Smart max. A ready card shows two numbers, like "Pordl target: 80 pitches · Pitch Smart max 95." The max is the official limit for his age. The Pordl target is Marcus's recommended count, set below the max. Aim for the target, and pull him sooner if he's tired or his mechanics slip.

The 14-day strip under the status shows the last two weeks. A filled bar is a game with its pitch count. A dashed outline is a bullpen. The solid outline is today.

Today's work and the arm check

Today's work is free for every family. It lists his routine for the day: throwing, running, lifting and arm care. It changes with his role, his last outing and his next game.

Answer How's his arm today? every day. It's one tap:

  • Good: normal routine.
  • Sore: no pitching today. Light throwing at most, plus arm care.
  • Pain: stop all throwing until he's checked by a doctor or athletic trainer.

Soreness is normal after pitching. Pain is not. Not sure which? Treat it as pain.

Starters: set Next start in the planner and the app lays out every day until then. Relievers: daily catch and sprints, short bullpens when he hasn't pitched in a while, and a day off after getting hot one day and pitching the next.

Logging and planning

Log every outing the same day. On Log outing, pick the date and type (Game, Bullpen, or Got hot for relievers), the team, pitches and innings (3.1 means 3⅓), and how his arm felt. Tap Save outing.

Only game pitches count toward rest. Log bullpens anyway to track his total throwing. Go over the Pordl target and the app reminds you to watch for fatigue. Go over the Pitch Smart max and you'll see a stronger warning.

Plan around the next game: set the date of his next game. The orange number shows how many pitches he can throw today and still be eligible for it.

Tournament planner

Use the Tournament tab before a big weekend to see who can pitch in every game, and how much.

  1. Under Add a game, enter the date, the time if you know it, and a name like "Pool play vs. Rangers." Tap Add game. Repeat for every game.
  2. Each game lists every pitcher with his max pitches for that game, or Out with the reason.
  3. Type a planned count in the Plan box. Every later game updates to match, so you can see what throwing him Friday does to Sunday.

Staff today at the top shows each pitcher's status at a glance.

The planner follows Pitch Smart rest days, the Pordl targets and the Pordl day-after rule: 40 pitches max the day after he pitches, and 90 over the two days. It never plans one game twice in a day or three days in a row. A pitcher with pain logged, shut down or still ramping up is out.

Done with the weekend? Tap Remove on a game or Clear tournament. The plan is a guide. If he's sore or his arm doesn't feel right, he doesn't pitch.

Teams: coaches and families

The Team tab links families to their coach. Coaches and parents use the same Sign in. Teams are free during launch.

For coaches

  1. Sign in, open Team, enter a team name under Coach a team and tap Create team.
  2. You get a 6-character join code. Text it to your families.
  3. Tap Open on your team to see every shared pitcher's status today. It updates as families log pitches. Tap Refresh for the latest.
  4. Plan tournament weekends for the whole staff right there. Team plans are kept separate from your own pitchers.
  5. Under Families you'll see each parent's email. Tap Remove to drop a family, or Delete team at season's end.

For families

  1. Open Team, enter the code from his coach under Join your team.
  2. Check which pitcher to share. Brothers on other teams stay private. Tap Join team.
  3. Keep logging outings as usual. His coach sees his pitch counts, rest and arm status but can't change anything.
  4. To share a different pitcher, enter the code again with other names checked. Tap Leave any time to stop sharing.

On several teams? Join each one with its code. Every coach sees the same log, so all his pitches count together.

Seasons, time off and teams

  • In-season: normal routine and pitch counts.
  • Off-season: shut down: no overhead throwing. Lift, run and play other sports.
  • Ramp-up: a step-by-step build back to games, throwing every other day: 4 weeks for ages 9–12, 6 for 13–14, 8 for 15–18.

Experts recommend at least 4 months off from throwing a year, 2–3 of them in a row. The app records his break when you switch him from shut down back to in-season.

Several teams? Log every team's games. The app adds them together. Also a catcher? Check the box and the app reminds you to avoid catching on days he isn't pitching.

Max and rest: MLB Pitch Smart. Pordl targets: Marcus McBeth.

  • Never pitch three days in a row.
  • Never pitch in more than one game in a day.
  • Stay under 100 innings in any 12 months.
  • No pitching when sore. No throwing at all with pain.

If his league or tournament has stricter limits, follow theirs.

FAQ

It says "Not saved: check connection."
You lost signal. Your entries stay on the device. Reconnect and tap anything, like the arm check, to save again.
My data isn't on my other device.
Sign in with the same email on both.
Do bullpens count toward rest?
No, only game pitches. Log bullpens anyway.
I logged something wrong.
Open History, tap × on the outing and log it again.
Is there a separate coach login?
No. Coaches sign in like everyone else and create a team on the Team tab.
Can his coach change my data?
No. Coaches can only view the pitchers you share.
How do I delete my account?
Open Pitchers, tap Delete account at the bottom and type DELETE. Everything is erased for good. Cancel any paid plan first under Plans.
I forgot my password.
Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.
