Privacy

Pordl Pitching ("we", "us") is run by Pordl. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We wrote it in plain English on purpose.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored encrypted by our sign-in provider; we never see them.
  • Pitcher details you enter: a name or nickname, age group, role, season, teams, pitch counts, innings, rest days, arm checks, velocity and notes.
  • Videos you upload for a review, and the review Marcus writes back.
  • Your plan status (free or paid, and when it renews).

We do not ask for a pitcher's full legal name, birth date, school, address or photo ID. A first name or nickname is enough.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show his status, track workload and build his plan.
  • To give video reviews on paid plans that include them.
  • To manage your subscription and answer your questions.

We do not sell your information, we do not run ads, and we do not share it with anyone for marketing.

Who helps us run the app

  • Supabase stores accounts, app data and videos.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Stripe handles payments. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

These companies only process data to provide their service to us.

Who can see videos

Only you and Marcus. Videos are stored privately and are never posted publicly or used in Pordl marketing unless you give separate written permission.

Keeping, changing and deleting

You can edit or remove a pitcher and his logs in the app at any time. To delete your whole account, videos included, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days. We may keep basic billing records where the law requires it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and each family can only see its own pitchers. No system is perfect; if we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

Kids

Pordl Pitching is for parents, guardians and coaches. Accounts must be opened by an adult, 18 or older. Players under 13 should not create their own account.

When you add a pitcher who is your child, or a player you coach with his parents' permission, you are telling us you have the right to share his information and videos with us. If you believe a child created an account without a parent, email us and we will delete it.

  • Only upload videos you have the right to share. Don't upload anything unrelated to pitching.
  • Pitch Smart limits shown in the app are guidelines from MLB and USA Baseball; Pordl targets are Marcus's own, more conservative numbers. Your league's rules always come first.

Paid plans, cancelling and refunds

  • The free account stays free. Paid plans are billed monthly at the price shown when you sign up, and renew automatically until you cancel.
  • Founding-member pricing stays at your signup price for as long as your subscription stays active. If you cancel and come back later, current pricing applies.
  • Cancel anytime from My plan → Manage subscription. You keep paid features until the end of the month you've paid for, then the account returns to free. Your logs stay.
  • Refunds: we don't give partial-month refunds. If you were charged by mistake or something isn't working, email us within 14 days of the charge and we will make it right.
  • Marcus aims to return video reviews within 3 days. Each plan's review allowance resets monthly and unused reviews don't roll over.
  • If we ever change a plan's price, we'll tell you at least 30 days before it applies to you.

Not medical advice

Pordl Pitching is a workload and training tool. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat injuries.

If he has pain, not normal soreness, stop throwing and see a doctor or athletic trainer. Pain at the inside of the elbow, pain that changes how he throws, numbness or tingling, or pain that lasts more than a day are reasons to get checked before he throws again. Always follow the advice of his doctor over anything in the app.
